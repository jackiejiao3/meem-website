Cooling Without Water Consumption

Air Cooled Heat Exchangers (ACHE) use ambient air as the cooling medium, completely eliminating the need for water consumption in process cooling and steam condensation applications. This makes them essential technology for power plants and industrial facilities located in water-scarce regions or where environmental regulations restrict water usage.

MEEM's ACHE product portfolio spans the full spectrum of air cooling technology — from compact finned tube coolers to massive modular air-cooled condenser systems capable of handling the exhaust from utility-scale steam turbines.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of air cooled heat exchangers does MEEM supply?

A: MEEM offers air finned coolers, air cooled condensers (ACC), modular ACHE units, hot air recirculation ACHE, and replacement tube bundles and finned tube elements.

Q: Why choose air cooling instead of water cooling?

A: Air cooled heat exchangers use ambient air as the cooling medium, eliminating water consumption. They are ideal for arid regions, desert power plants and projects with strict environmental water-use restrictions.

Q: What capacity range can MEEM's air cooled condensers cover?

A: MEEM's air cooled condenser systems can be engineered for steam turbine applications from 10 MW to over 1,000 MW.

Q: Can MEEM supply replacement parts for existing ACHE units?

A: Yes. MEEM provides OEM-quality replacement tube bundles and finned tube elements, including reverse engineering when original drawings are unavailable.

Q: What standards do MEEM's ACHE replacement bundles meet?

A: Replacement tube bundles and finned tube elements are manufactured to comply with API 661 standards.
