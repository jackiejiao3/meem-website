Corporate Governance

Upholding transparency, accountability, and ethical standards in every facet of our business.

Built on Integrity and Accountability

MEEM operates under a robust corporate governance framework that aligns with international best practices and the standards expected by our global customer base. As a member of the Modern Group's manufacturing network, we adhere to consistent governance policies that ensure transparency, ethical conduct, and regulatory compliance.

Our governance structure covers quality assurance systems, supply chain ethics, data protection, anti-corruption measures, and continuous compliance monitoring across all business functions.

Framework

Governance Principles

01

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Rigorous QA/QC protocols governed by ASME code requirements and ISO 9001 standards. Independent third-party inspections, full material traceability, and documented procedures at every stage of fabrication from raw material receipt to final delivery.

02

Supply Chain Ethics

Comprehensive supplier qualification program including vendor audits, material source verification, and ethical sourcing guidelines. We only partner with suppliers who meet our standards for quality, reliability, and responsible business practices.

03

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

Dual certification under ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) and ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety). Regular internal and external audits, incident reporting systems, and continuous improvement initiatives driven by HSE performance metrics.

04

Transparency & Documentation

Complete project documentation packages including design calculations, material certificates (MTC), welding records, NDT reports, hydro-test results, and as-built documentation. Full digital archiving ensures long-term traceability for every vessel or module delivered.

05

Business Ethics & Integrity

Zero-tolerance policy toward corruption, bribery, and conflicts of interest. Clear codes of conduct for employees, vendors, and partners. Confidentiality agreements and intellectual property protections in place for all customer projects.

06

Risk Management

Proactive risk identification and mitigation framework covering technical risks (design changes, material availability), commercial risks (currency fluctuation, contract terms), and operational risks (capacity planning, workforce management).
