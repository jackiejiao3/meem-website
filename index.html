Who We Are

About MEEM

A Global Leader in Energy Equipment Manufacturing

Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM) is a premier manufacturer specializing in high-quality, custom-engineered equipment for the global energy sector. With state-of-the-art facilities and a team of experienced professionals, we deliver innovative solutions that meet the most demanding industry standards.

From heat recovery steam generators to advanced finned tube heat exchangers, our products serve combined-cycle power plants, industrial facilities, and renewable energy installations around the world.

Our Location

Want to Visit Us?

MEEM — the third manufacturing facility of Modern Group — is located in Zhangjiagang, a thriving industrial city along the Yangtze River in Jiangsu Province. Our facility benefits from exceptional transport connectivity: approximately 2.5 hours by car to Shanghai (PVG / Hongqiao), 1 hour by car to Wuxi Shuofang International Airport, and only 30 minutes by car to Zhangjiagang High-Speed Railway Station. Whether you arrive by air, rail, or road, getting to MEEM is convenient from anywhere in the Yangtze River Delta.

Shanghai Airports PVG & Hongqiao — ~2.5h
Wuxi Shuofang International Airport — ~1h
Zhangjiagang HSR High-Speed Railway — ~30 min
