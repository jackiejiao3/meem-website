Design & Detailing

Connecting customer design with manufacturing execution.

Explore Our Approach
Built Around Manufacturing

Turning Customer Models into Manufacturing Detail

MEEM develops customer-supplied models, drawings, and technical requirements into detailed documentation for fabrication and assembly. Working within the agreed project scope, our team refines component details, coordinates interfaces, and prepares manufacturing drawings that connect engineering intent with shop-floor execution.

With detailing and production teams working closely together, we address fabrication questions, coordinate revisions, and support the transition from customer design to manufactured equipment.

Our Scope

Detailing That Supports Production

Practical coordination at each stage, from model development to shop-floor clarification.

01

Model Development & Coordination

We develop and refine customer-provided models for fabrication, coordinating component arrangements, connection details, and assembly interfaces. Gaps and discrepancies are reviewed with the customer’s engineering team before production.

02

Fabrication & Assembly Drawings

We prepare detailed shop and assembly drawings from the agreed design inputs, defining dimensions, component details, connections, and assembly information to guide manufacturing.

03

Manufacturability Review

Our detailing work considers welding access, fit-up, fabrication sequence, and assembly constraints. Proposed adjustments are coordinated with the customer while maintaining the agreed design requirements.

04

Production Engineering Support

We support fabrication and assembly by clarifying drawing details, responding to technical queries, and incorporating agreed revisions into manufacturing documentation.
