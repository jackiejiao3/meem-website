Heat Recovery Steam Generator

Complete HRSG module solutions serving combined-cycle and conventional power plants worldwide.

The Heart of Combined-Cycle Power Generation

Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSG) are critical components in modern combined-cycle power plants, capturing waste heat from gas turbine exhaust to generate steam that drives additional turbines — significantly boosting overall plant thermal efficiency.

At MEEM, we manufacture the complete range of HRSG components and assemblies, from individual finned tubes and headers to fully integrated shop-assembled modules. Our products serve both new construction projects and retrofit/replacement applications globally.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is a Heat Recovery Steam Generator (HRSG)?

A: An HRSG captures waste heat from gas turbines or industrial processes to generate steam. It is widely used in combined-cycle power plants and industrial waste heat recovery systems to improve overall thermal efficiency.

Q: What HRSG products does MEEM manufacture?

A: MEEM produces finned tubes, headers and manifolds, harps and platens, and fully shop-assembled HRSG modules including economizers, evaporators, superheaters and reheaters.

Q: What materials does MEEM use for HRSG components?

A: MEEM works with carbon steel, low-alloy steel, stainless steel and nickel alloys, selected according to operating temperature, pressure and corrosion conditions.

Q: What standards do MEEM's HRSG products comply with?

A: MEEM designs and manufactures HRSG components in accordance with ASME Section I and ASME Section VIII Division 1 and 2.

Q: Can MEEM deliver pre-assembled HRSG modules?

A: Yes. MEEM provides shop-assembled modules that reduce field installation time by up to 40% and lower total installed cost.
