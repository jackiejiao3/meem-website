ASME Certification

ASME Pressure Equipment Stamps

MEEM holds ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) certification for the design and fabrication of power boilers, pressure vessels, and related pressure-retaining equipment.

S

ASME S Stamp — Power Boilers

Authorization to fabricate power boiler components under ASME Section I rules. This stamp covers HRSG pressure part fabrication including steam drums, headers, and economizer modules for utility and industrial combined-cycle power plants.

  • Steam drum fabrication
  • Header manufacturing
  • Economizer & evaporator assemblies
  • HRSG module assembly
Cert #63092 Mar 2026 – Mar 2029 View Full
U

ASME U Stamp — Pressure Vessels

Full authorization to fabricate pressure vessels under ASME Section VIII Division 1 rules. Covers a wide range of vessel types including process vessels, separators, reactors, storage tanks, and heat exchanger shells for petrochemical and industrial applications.

  • Process & reactor vessels
  • Separator & accumulator tanks
  • Storage vessels
  • Heat exchanger shells & bundles
Cert #63091 Mar 2026 – Mar 2029 View Full
PP

ASME PP Stamp — Piping

Authorization to manufacture piping systems under ASME B31.1 Power Piping and B31.3 Process Piping codes. Enables fabrication of complete piping spools, manifolds, and interconnecting piping assemblies for power plant and process facility construction projects.

  • Power piping (B31.1) fabrication
  • Process piping (B31.3) fabrication
  • Manifold & header assemblies
  • Pipe spool prefabrication
Cert #63093 Mar 2026 – Mar 2029 View Full
