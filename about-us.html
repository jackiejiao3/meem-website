Who We Are

The Modern Group

The Third Manufacturing Facility of Modern Group

Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM) is the third manufacturing facility established by the Modern Group, strategically positioned to expand the group's capabilities in specialized energy equipment fabrication.

Building on decades of collective expertise from sister facilities, MEEM focuses exclusively on three core product lines: Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSG), Air Cooled Heat Exchangers, and Pressure Vessels. This focused specialization allows us to deliver unparalleled quality and efficiency in every project we undertake.

Our Expertise

Core Focus Areas

MEEM concentrates its manufacturing excellence on three strategic product categories, each representing critical equipment in modern power generation and industrial processes.

Heat Recovery Steam Generator

Complete HRSG solutions from individual components to fully integrated module assemblies. We manufacture finned tubes, headers, harps, and shop-assembled modules for combined-cycle and conventional power plants worldwide.

Learn More

Air Cooled Heat Exchanger

Comprehensive air-cooled heat transfer solutions for power generation and process industries. From air finned coolers to complete modular condenser systems designed for water-scarce regions and extreme ambient conditions.

Learn More

Pressure Vessels

Custom-designed pressure vessels engineered to strict safety codes including ASME Section VIII Div. 1 & 2. Serving petrochemical, power generation, and industrial processing applications with full material traceability.

Why Choose MEEM?

Group Synergy

As part of the Modern Group's growing network of manufacturing facilities, we benefit from shared technical resources, proven management systems, and cross-facility expertise accumulated over years of industrial experience.
