Project Gallery

A showcase of completed projects demonstrating our manufacturing capabilities across all product lines.

Projects That Speak for Themselves

Since establishing our facility at Yangtze River Heavy Equipment Port, MEEM has successfully delivered a wide range of projects for domestic and international clients across the power generation, petrochemical, and industrial processing sectors.

Each project in our gallery represents a unique set of engineering challenges — from complex HRSG module assemblies requiring multi-axis precision fit-up to massive air-cooled condenser structures designed for extreme ambient conditions. Our track record demonstrates consistent delivery performance, code compliance, and customer satisfaction.

Four Key Manufacturing Stages

HRSG Component Manufacturing & Assembly

Our HRSG manufacturing capabilities cover header fabrication, spiral finned tube manufacturing, harp and platen assembly, and module assembly.

Headers

Header Fabrication

HRSG headers are fabricated with tube connection openings in accordance with project drawings, ready for tube-to-header assembly.

Headers Tube Connections Fabrication
Spiral Finned Tubes

Spiral Finned Tube Manufacturing

Spiral fins increase the external heat-transfer surface of HRSG tubes. Tubes and fins are manufactured to project specifications for subsequent panel assembly.

Spiral Finned Tubes Extended Surface Tube Manufacturing
Harp & Platen

Harp & Platen Assembly

Finned tubes are assembled into harp and platen panels, with alignment and tube-to-header connections completed in accordance with project drawings.

Finned Tube Panels Tube Alignment Panel Assembly
Module Assembly

HRSG Module Assembly

HRSG tube panels are positioned, aligned and assembled into modules in accordance with project drawings.

Module Assembly Alignment Component Integration
